Order of Service

Understanding the Flow and Meaning of Celestial Worship

  • Overview
  • Order of Service
  • The Mercy Land
  • The Sutana
  • Sanctuary & Altar
  • Prophetic Ministry

Worship in Celestial Church of Christ follows an established order. From the opening procession and prayers to Scripture, hymns, the sermon, thanksgiving, and closing, each part of the service has a place within the worship life of the Church.

This guide is being developed to do more than show what happens next. It will also explain the meaning behind the prayers, Psalms, responses, actions, and Celestial expressions heard throughout the service.

Service Guides

Coming Soon

Sunday Service

A step-by-step guide to the Lord's Day Service, explaining the order of worship and the meaning and biblical foundation behind its prayers, Psalms, hymns, responses, and sacred actions.

Coming Soon

Weekday Service

A guide to the order used during our weekday devotional services, including Mercy Day and Power Day, with explanations of the prayers, Psalms, hymns, responses, and worship practices found throughout the service.

More Than an Order of Events

The completed guides will help worshippers understand not only what happens during a Celestial service, but why.

Topics will include the meaning behind Celestial expressions and responses, the purpose of particular Psalms and prayers, the ringing of the bell and congregational responses, the use of hymns, Scripture readings, thanksgiving, and other parts of the established Order of Service.

Each part will be explained using the Church's teaching and its biblical foundation where applicable.
