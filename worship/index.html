Worship

Worship

Worshipping God with reverence, order, and devotion.

Worship is at the heart of the life of the Church. Celestians gather to pray, sing, hear the Word of God, give thanks, give offerings, and seek the presence of the Spirit of the Lord.

Worship is approached with reverence and within an established order. The different ministries and elements of the service work together to help the congregation worship God and participate together.

Gathered to Worship

Prayer, hymns and spiritual songs, Scripture, preaching, Praise & Worship, thanksgiving, offerings, spiritual ministry, and congregational participation all have a place within the worship of the Church.

Each element has its place within the worship of the Church, and the service is conducted according to an established order.

What to Expect in Sunday Worship

Sunday worship follows an established order. The general sequence below is drawn from the Church's own description of the service — it's meant to help a first-time visitor know roughly what to expect, not to promise a minute-by-minute guarantee. Individual services are led according to this order, but the details of any one service are at the discretion of those leading it.

Gathering & Procession

Attendees queue outside the Sanctuary before the service begins. The service conductor opens in prayer, and a processional hymn plays as everyone proceeds inside.

Standing, Kneeling & the Bell

Everyone remains standing until the seven candles on the altar are lit and the Inner Altar is sanctified. At that point, everyone kneels. At the jingle of the bell, the whole congregation bows down together, saying, “Holy, Holy, Holy is the Lord God of Hosts.”

Psalms, Prayers & Hymns

A cyclical session of Psalm recitations, prayers, and hymns follows, according to the Church's Order of Service.

Bible Readings, Announcements & Sermon

The congregation is then seated for two Bible readings (with a Gloria interlude and song between them), announcements, and the sermon.

Collections, Tithe & Thanksgiving

The service proceeds to collections and tithe offering. Once that's complete, those bringing a thanksgiving offering come forward with their offering trays, dancing and singing from the entrance of the Sanctuary.

Closing

A closing hymn and prayers bring the service to an end, and attendees queue back out of the Sanctuary.

Worship With Reverence

Reverence is reflected not only in prayer and song, but also in how we conduct ourselves during worship. Celestians are expected to maintain appropriate behavior, respect the Sanctuary, and avoid actions that distract from worship.

The Word of God

Scripture and the preaching of God's Word hold an important place within worship. The congregation gathers not only to sing and pray, but also to hear, receive, and reflect upon God's Word.

Read About Our Faith

Prayer in Worship

Prayer is woven throughout the spiritual life of the Church. Congregational prayer gives the Church an opportunity to seek God together, give thanks, ask for His direction, and bring the needs of His people before Him.

Music & Congregational Praise

Music helps the congregation participate actively in worship. Hymns, spiritual songs, Praise & Worship, and Thanksgiving songs each have a place within the worship life of the Church.

Learn About the Ministry of the Choir

Giving as Worship

Giving is also an expression of worship. Seed Offerings, Tithes, and Thanksgiving Offerings each have a distinct place within the spiritual life of the Church.

Spiritual Gifts Within Order

Prophetic ministry has a recognized place within the Church, but spiritual gifts are exercised within established order and with self-control.

The Church's guidance emphasizes that prophetic activity should not disrupt worship or override the established order of the service.

The Sanctuary

The Sanctuary is arranged according to established Church practice and contains distinctive elements that contribute to the worship environment.

Worship Is Something We Do Together

Worship is not simply something the congregation watches. Celestians participate through prayer, singing, listening, giving, thanksgiving, and reverent attention.

The Choir serves the congregation. Ministers serve the congregation. Leaders serve the congregation. And the congregation worships together.

Worship Beyond Sunday

Worship and spiritual life continue beyond the Sunday service. Disciple Parish also gathers for additional services and spiritual activities throughout the week.

Sunday Devotional, Mercy Day, and Power Day each have their place in the worship life of Disciple Parish.
