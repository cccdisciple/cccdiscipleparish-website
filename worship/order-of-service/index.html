Wednesday & Friday Evening Service Mercy Day · Power Day View Order

Wednesday is Mercy Day. Friday is Power Day. Both follow the same Order of Service, with certain hymn themes reflecting the emphasis of each day.

What Is the Order of Service?

The Order of Service is the sequence of prayers, Psalms, hymns, responses, and sacred actions revealed for worship in Celestial Church of Christ.
