Worship

Spiritual Work

Seeking God through prayer, faith, spiritual discipline, and dependence upon the Spirit of the Lord.

Spiritual Work is part of the spiritual life of Celestians. It is approached within a life of prayer, faith, spiritual discipline, and dependence upon God.

What Is Spiritual Work?

Spiritual Work refers to established forms of spiritual service and practice through which believers seek God, His direction, His help, and His intervention in the circumstances of life.

Prayer & Spiritual Work

Prayer remains central to the spiritual life of the believer. Spiritual Work is approached alongside prayer and faith rather than as a replacement for a person's relationship with God.

Seeking the Spirit of the Lord

Celestians seek the leading of the Spirit of the Lord in their spiritual lives. Spiritual Work is approached with reverence, faith, and dependence upon God.

Fasting & Spiritual Discipline

Fasting and other forms of spiritual discipline may accompany a believer's spiritual life as they seek God with greater intentionality.

When Life Gets Complicated

Life can bring circumstances that require prayer, guidance, patience, and spiritual support. Celestians may turn to God during times of difficulty and seek appropriate spiritual support within the life of the Church.

Spiritual Guidance & Discernment

Spiritual matters should be approached with discernment and within the established order of the Church. Believers should seek appropriate guidance rather than acting impulsively or treating spiritual experiences casually.

A Life Beyond the Sanctuary

Spiritual life does not end when worship ends. Prayer, holiness, gratitude, Christian conduct, service, and dependence upon God continue throughout daily life.

Spiritual Work & Christian Life

Spiritual Work belongs within a broader Christian life of faith, prayer, worship, holiness, thanksgiving, and service.
