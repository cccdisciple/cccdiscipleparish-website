New Here

Welcome

We're glad you're thinking of joining us. Here's what to expect when you visit Disciple Parish.

What to Expect

A warm welcome, heartfelt worship, and a place to belong.

Worship

Our services include prayer, hymns, and the preaching of God's Word, offered with reverence and joy.

Prayer & Spiritual Work

Prayer is central to who we are. You'll find prayer woven throughout our services, along with space to bring your own needs before God.

Music & Hymns

Hymns and worship songs are an important part of how we gather to praise God together.

Attire

The white prayer-and-worship garment worn by Celestians is called the Sutana (SU-TA-NA). You do not need to own a Sutana to visit. Come as you are, and feel free to ask questions as you become familiar with Celestial worship.

A Welcoming Environment

Whether it's your first time in a church or you've worshipped your whole life in a different tradition, you are welcome here.
