About

Church Life & Leadership

Growing together in worship, service, fellowship, and spiritual order.

The Church is more than a place where services take place. It is a community of believers who worship together, serve one another, grow in faith, and share responsibility for the life of the congregation.

Life Together

Church life includes worship, fellowship, prayer, service, instruction, thanksgiving, and the everyday relationships that connect believers to one another.

Parish Spiritual Order & Shared Responsibility

The Church maintains an established order for worship, ministry, leadership, and conduct. This order helps the congregation serve together with reverence, discipline, and unity.

The Pastor serves as the head of the Church worldwide. At different levels of the Church's structure, the Pastor is represented by designated leaders, including the Shepherd at the parish level and supervisors and heads at district, state, regional, provincial, and diocesan levels.

At Disciple Parish, the Shepherd represents the Pastor and provides leadership for the parish.

The Shepherd's Role

Leadership within the Church carries responsibility. Those who serve are expected to approach their responsibilities with humility, discipline, integrity, and a commitment to the spiritual well-being of the congregation.

Within a parish, the Shepherd represents the Pastor and serves as head of the parish in all matters — guiding the Parochial Committee, giving final approval before its decisions are carried out, and remaining the parish's link to the wider Church. The Shepherd is also responsible for the conduct of services and sermons, for organizing spiritual occasions such as baptisms, weddings, and Naming Ceremonies, for counseling members (or delegating this to capable elders), and for conducting or delegating the Service of Prophets and prophetesses.

The Parochial Committee

A Parochial Committee is part of a parish's administration — a group of relatively senior, experienced members who assist and advise the Shepherd, without taking over the Shepherd's authority. Committee size can range from about seven to as many as thirty-three members, depending on the size of the congregation, and members typically serve a term of two to three years.

Members are chosen either by spiritual balloting, where a parish is of one spirit, accord, and love, or by democratic secret ballot conducted by independent electoral officers. No decision of the committee is implemented without the Shepherd's approval.

Serving Others

Church life is not only about receiving. Members are encouraged to contribute through their time, abilities, resources, prayer, encouragement, and practical service.

Responsibilities Within the Church

Different ministries and responsibilities contribute to the life of the Church. The Choir, prophetic ministry, worship leadership, wardens, and other forms of service work together within the established order of the congregation, alongside the Shepherd and Parochial Committee described above.

Respect & Christian Conduct

The life of a congregation depends upon the way its members treat one another. Respect, self-control, honesty, peace, humility, and Christian love help strengthen the community.

Learning & Growing

Growth in faith includes learning. Bible study, instruction, preaching, prayer, and fellowship provide opportunities for believers to deepen their understanding of Scripture and grow in their walk with God.

Church Life Beyond Services

Church community continues beyond formal worship services. Fellowship, service, prayer, encouragement, and care for one another are part of living as a community of believers.

Disciple Parish

Disciple Parish is a local congregation of Celestial Church of Christ. Our goal is to provide a place where people can worship God, grow in faith, serve others, and experience Christian fellowship.
