About This Reference

This guide documents the rank structure of Celestial Church of Christ (CCC) — published by CCC Disciple Parish as an educational reference to the denomination-wide system, not a hierarchy created by or limited to this parish.

Last reviewed: September 2026

Understanding the CCC Rank System

Celestial Church of Christ has an established hierarchy of spiritual ranks, with recognized rank lines for men, women, Prophets, and Prophetesses. Three related concepts are helpful when understanding the structure.

Spiritual Rank

A member’s recognized place within the rank hierarchy of Celestial Church of Christ.

Prophetic Calling

Prophets and Prophetesses have their own recognized rank lines within CCC. A prophetic calling remains distinct from a person’s progression through other ranks.

Clergy Status

CCC distinguishes between clergy and non-clergy. This distinction is especially important within portions of the higher rank structure, where parallel ranks distinguish clergy from non-clergy members.

Sources & Methodology

This reference is built from the Church’s own governing documents and institutional publications, corroborated where possible by contemporary CCC Worldwide material.

Primary / Governing CCC Material

  1. Constitution of Celestial Church of Christ
  2. Pastorate / Office of the Pastor directives
  3. Pastor-in-Council / Pastoral Council orders and official rank directives
  4. Official CCC institutional publications

Supporting Evidence

  1. Identifiable high-ranking CCC officials and institutional teaching
  2. Established CCC historical material
  3. Academic research specifically documenting CCC
  4. Established parish/institutional publications, used for corroboration

Social-media and member material may be used for discovery or corroboration of current title usage, but it is not presented here as equivalent to a Pastorate directive or the Constitution. Historical rank lists available online may not reflect later developments in the Higher Evangelist Orders; this reference incorporates later Pastorate directives and contemporary CCC institutional documentation where available.

The CCC Constitution establishes that the Church has a hierarchy, that different ranks are distinguished through robes, and that members are directed to the Church publication Order of Seniority and Promotion and Dressing Rank by Rank and its accompanying chart (Clauses 185–187). Academic documentation identifying this publication as CCC Pastoral Council Order No. 4 further supports its provenance. A copy of the Constitution used for this research is available below.
