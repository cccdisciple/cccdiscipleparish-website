Leadership & Ranks

Celestial Church of Christ Men's Ranks

The general male rank progression in Celestial Church of Christ, continuing into the Higher Evangelist Orders, with the Prophetic line shown as a related path.

  • Leadership & Ranks
  • Men's Ranks
  • Women's Ranks

Read down the list to see the rank progression. Select any rank for its position and any additional approved detail.

Ranks in Celestial Church of Christ reflect an established order of seniority, service, responsibility, and distinctive Sutana. The progression shown here helps visitors understand the Church’s rank structure, but it should not be understood as an automatic ladder in which every member simply advances through every rank. Higher appointments are governed by the order and authority of the Church.

Last reviewed: September 2026

Common Starting Rank

  1. Brother
    Next
    General Line or Prophetic Line, below

    The beginning rank within the male leadership line. A Brother wears the standard Brother Sutana.

    Every man begins as Brother. A member with a recognized prophetic calling follows the related Prophetic Line instead of the General Line.

    The Superior Senior Leader wears a laced yellow short-wing upper garment with a blue crossbar and yellow strands, together with a white loin bearing three blue crosses and yellow strands.

    The Constitution identifies Superior Senior Leader, Superior Senior Wolida, and Superior Senior Elder as corresponding senior ranks. Advancement to Assistant Evangelist and above is not described as automatic merely because a member has reached one of these ranks.

Prophetic Line

Members with a recognized prophetic calling follow this line instead of the General Line. A person's later elevation does not necessarily erase the prophetic calling — CCC may identify a senior member by both an Evangelist rank and a prophetic designation, for example Most Senior Evangelist (Prophet).

  1. Prophet
    Previous
    Brother
    Next
    Cape Elder Prophet

    The Prophet wears the male four-corner Sutana, reflecting his recognized place within the prophetic line of the Church.

  2. Cape Elder Prophet
    Previous

    Next
    Woleader

    The Cape Elder Prophet is distinguished by the four-corner Sutana with an unlaced cape, a blue loin bearing one white cross, and no strands.

    Sometimes referred to by the shortened usage “Cape Prophet”; this page uses the fuller public-facing title Cape Elder Prophet.

  3. Woleader
    Previous
    Cape Elder Prophet

    Senior Woleader

    The Woleader wears the four-corner Sutana with an unlaced cape and an unlaced white upper garment. The blue loin bears three white crosses.

    Alternate English spellings such as Wolidah, Wolida, and Wolider are sometimes encountered for this title. This website standardizes on Woleader.

  4. Senior Woleader
    Previous
    Woleader
    Next

  5. Superior Senior Woleader
    Previous
    Senior Woleader
    Next
    Higher Evangelist Orders (by selection)

    The Superior Senior Woleader wears a laced cape with a laced blue upper garment, a blue loin bearing three white crosses, and white strands.

    Superior Senior Woleader is the recognized senior prophetic rank corresponding to Superior Senior Leader and Superior Senior Elder. As with the General Male Rank Line, advancement from here into the Higher Evangelist Orders is by selection, not automatic succession.

The General and Prophetic lines are distinct from clergy status. Members reaching the Higher Evangelist Orders may enter the applicable clergy or non-clergy rank structure according to Church selection and status.

Historical & Exceptional — Outside the Ordinary Progression

Historical & Exceptional

Supreme Evangelist

The Supreme Evangelist wears a white cassock with a long deep-blue robe and a deep-purple crossbar with light-purple strands. The deep-blue loin bears the purple Altar-cross design, with three crosses at the front and three at each end, finished with purple strands.

Supreme Evangelist was a unique and exceptional rank in the history of Celestial Church of Christ. According to official CCC Worldwide founder/history material, Pastor-Founder S.B.J. Oshoffa appointed Rev. Alexander Abiodun Bada the first and only Supreme Evangelist on December 25, 1980, describing him at that time as second in rank to the Pastor-Founder. Rev. Bada later became Pastor and Supreme Head of Celestial Church of Christ Worldwide following Pastor-Founder Oshoffa’s death.

Supreme Evangelist should not be understood as an ordinary promotion through which every senior Evangelist must pass, nor does holding the title itself automatically make a person the Pastor. Pastor & Supreme Head is likewise an office of worldwide headship, not an automatic next step from Supreme Evangelist or Most Superior Evangelist.

Worldwide Office & Headship

Pastor & Supreme Head, Celestial Church of Christ Worldwide

The Pastor is the worldwide head of Celestial Church of Christ. The documented ranking system identifies the Pastor’s garment as the Pastoral Tunic.

This is an office of worldwide headship and should not be presented as an automatic promotion following Supreme Evangelist or Most Superior Evangelist.
