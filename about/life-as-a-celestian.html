Life as a Celestian

Life as a Celestian is rooted in Christian faith and a desire to honor God in worship and daily life.

Life as a Celestian is rooted in Christian faith and a desire to honor God in worship and daily life. Within Celestial Church of Christ, members follow established practices and expectations that guide preparation for worship, conduct within the Sanctuary, reverence, appearance, and other areas of church life.

These teachings are not simply rules to be read. They guide how we worship, how we conduct ourselves in the House of God, and how we seek to live our daily lives as followers of Jesus Christ.

Our Tenets

Practices That Are Forbidden

Members of Celestial Church of Christ are forbidden from participating in idolatry, fetish practices or cults, black magic, magical charms, tarot card reading, and other practices associated with powers of darkness.

Smoking cigarettes or tobacco, smoking or using weed, and the consumption of alcohol, wine, or other intoxicating drinks are also forbidden.

Fornication and adultery are forbidden.

Members are called to turn away from practices that conflict with Christian life and to seek holiness in body and soul.

Food & Dietary Restrictions

Members are forbidden from eating pork, blood, food offered as a sacrifice to idols or other powers of darkness, crawling animals such as snakes and snails, and species of fish that have no scales.

Christian Conduct

Infighting is prohibited. Corrupt, abusive, or inappropriate speech has no place in the House of God.

Members should treat one another with respect and seek to live lives that reflect holiness, prayer, discipline, love, and obedience to God.

Attire & Appearance

The Sutana

The white prayer-and-worship garment worn by Celestians is known as the Sutana (SU-TA-NA).

Members do not wear shoes while wearing the Sutana or within the Church premises. Barefoot worship is part of the established order and practice of Celestial Church of Christ.

Learn more about the Sutana in Celestial Worship

Black & Red Apparel

Members refrain from wearing black and red apparel as part of the Church's established discipline and order.

Black is associated with mourning and sorrow. As we come into God's presence, our attire should reflect life, holiness, and the joy of salvation in Christ Jesus.

Red is traditionally associated with danger, warning, bloodshed, and spiritual warfare. Members avoid it as part of maintaining reverence, peace, and purity in worship.

Professional, occupational, and school requirements are recognized as exceptions. Members may wear black or red when required for work, official responsibilities, or a school uniform.

Head Covering

Female members keep their heads covered while within the Church and its premises.

Makeup

Female members who choose to wear makeup should keep it moderate while within the Church and its premises.

Sanctification & Purification

Preparing for Worship

Members are expected to keep themselves sanctified for 24 hours before Church services.

During this period, members are encouraged to prepare themselves for worship by maintaining purity in their conduct, avoiding conflict and inappropriate language, and remaining mindful of their spiritual preparation for the House of God.

Altar Elders are expected to observe this period of sanctification for 48 hours before Church services.

This time of preparation encourages members to approach worship with reverence, discipline, and a heart focused on God.

New Members

New members ordinarily undergo sanctification before being admitted as worshippers.

Sanctification is conducted with a bucket of water, sponge, soap, and a candle under the guidance of an authorized Elder.

New members should also be taught the established practices and expectations of Celestial Church of Christ as they become familiar with life in the Church.

Menstrual Purification

Female members during their monthly cycle do not enter the Church premises or handle holy items.

After seven clear days, sanctification takes place before returning.

If the cycle continues beyond seven days, two clear days after it stops are observed before sanctification.

Purification After Childbirth

Following childbirth, female members observe a 40-day period of purification before returning to the Church premises and undergo sanctification before re-entry.

Worship & Sanctuary Conduct

Reverence in the House of God

Upon entering the Church, members should remain conscious of the presence of God.

The Sanctuary is a place of prayer, worship, reverence, and order. Members should approach worship with respect for God and consideration for others who have come to pray and worship.

Seating During Worship

Male and female members do not sit side by side during worship.

The Altar

Female members do not enter the Altar area or lead the congregation in worship.

Male members entering the Altar are expected to wear the loin as part of the established Altar attire.

Learn about the Sanctuary & Altar

Cell Phones

All cell phones should be turned off or placed on silent while in the Sanctuary.

We understand that some individuals may need to remain reachable because of the nature of their work or other responsibilities. However, phones should remain on silent so that they do not disrupt worship or those around them.

During the Service

Talking and unnecessary discussion should not take place while a service is in progress. Irrelevant conversation should not take place in the Altar or elsewhere in the Sanctuary.

There should be no unnecessary interruption or interjection during the flow of worship, particularly during prayers and the sermon.

Spraying Money

Spraying money is not permitted in the Sanctuary.

Giving and thanksgiving take place according to the established order of worship.

Learn about Offerings & Giving

Praise & Worship

Praise and worship within the Sanctuary should be directed toward God. The Choir should not conduct praise worship of an individual within the Sanctuary.

The Bell

When the bell is jingled, members do not need to rush into the Sanctuary.

Wherever they may be within the Church premises, members should kneel and bow their heads in reverence to God.

Candles

Only white candles are used in Celestial Church of Christ. Colored candles are not permitted.

Prophetic Ministry & Worship Order

Prophetic ministry operates within the established order of worship.

Prophetic messages should not interrupt prayers or the sermon. When prophetic communication is necessary, it should take place according to the established order of the Church.

Learn about Prophetic Ministry

Biblical Foundations

Understanding the Biblical Foundations of Our Worship

Many practices within Celestial Church of Christ are connected to examples, instructions, and patterns found throughout Scripture.

Living What We Learn

The tenets and ordinances of Celestial Church of Christ are not meant simply to be read. Members are encouraged to understand them, practice them, and allow the principles of holiness, reverence, discipline, and Christian conduct to shape their daily lives.

As we enter the House of God, we remain mindful of His presence and seek to approach Him with reverence, humility, and good conduct.

May the Spirit of our Lord Jesus Christ continue to guide us as we worship and serve God together.
