Our Faith

Faith in Jesus Christ. Guided by the Holy Spirit. Rooted in Scripture.

At Disciple Parish, we are a Christian community centered on Jesus Christ and committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, and living lives that honor Him.

As Celestians, our faith is expressed through prayer, worship, Scripture, holiness, service, thanksgiving, and a continual desire to follow the leading of the Spirit of the Lord.

Faith in Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is at the center of our faith. We seek to know Him, follow Him, and live according to His teachings.

Guided by the Holy Spirit

We seek the leading of the Spirit of the Lord in our worship, prayer, spiritual life, and daily walk with God.

Rooted in Scripture

Scripture provides an essential foundation for our faith and our understanding of Christian life and worship.

A Life of Holiness

We are called to live lives that honor God through holiness, prayer, discipline, love, service, and Christian conduct.

Worship

Worship is at the heart of our life together. Through prayer, Scripture, music, preaching, thanksgiving, giving, and other expressions of devotion, we gather to honor God and seek His presence.

Learn About Our Worship

A Life of Prayer

Prayer is part of the believer's ongoing relationship with God. Through prayer we give thanks, seek guidance, bring our concerns before God, and remain attentive to His presence.

Holiness

Holiness involves more than what happens during worship. It is reflected in the choices we make and the way we conduct ourselves. To pursue holiness is to continually seek to live in a manner that honors God.

The Condition of the Heart

Christian conduct begins within. Love, peace, humility, sincerity, self-control, forgiveness, and faithfulness shape the way we respond to God and to other people.

A Christian life cannot be separated from the way we treat other people. Love, patience, forgiveness, compassion, honesty, and peace belong in our families, friendships, workplaces, and communities.

Our Christian Life

Faith extends beyond the Sanctuary. We seek to live our faith through prayer, fasting, thanksgiving, generosity, Christian conduct, service, and the way we treat others.

Prayer

Remaining in communication with God.

Fasting

Practicing spiritual discipline and seeking God.

Thanksgiving

Recognizing God's goodness and giving thanks.

Tithes & Offerings

Giving as an expression of worship and stewardship.

Christian Conduct

Allowing our faith to shape the way we live and treat others.

Service to Others

Using our time, abilities, and resources to serve God and people.

Generosity & Stewardship

Christian stewardship includes how we use our resources, time, abilities, and opportunities. Giving is one expression of that stewardship, while generosity also appears through service, hospitality, compassion, and willingness to help others.

Understand Our Offerings

Faith That Serves

Christian life is not only about receiving from the Church. It is also about finding ways to serve God and serve others. Every person has something they can contribute through time, abilities, resources, encouragement, prayer, or practical service.

You do not need a title to serve.

Faith Beyond the Sanctuary

What happens in worship should shape what happens afterward.

The reverence we bring into the Sanctuary should influence how we speak.

The prayers we offer should influence how we respond to difficulty.

The gratitude we express should influence how we see what we have.

The love we receive from God should influence how we treat others.

Living Out Our Faith

Being a Celestian is more than belonging to a congregation. It involves living a Christian life centered on faith in God, worship, prayer, holiness, service, and fellowship with other believers.
