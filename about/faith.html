About

Our Faith

What we believe, and how it shapes the way we worship and live together at Disciple Parish.

Faith in Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is the center of our worship and our lives. We believe He is Lord and Savior, and everything we do as a church flows from our devotion to Him.

Guided by the Holy Spirit

We seek the leading of the Holy Spirit in our worship, our prayer, and our daily lives, trusting Him to guide, comfort, and empower God's people.

Rooted in Scripture

God's Word is central to our teaching and worship. We look to Scripture to shape our faith and to guide how we live.

A Life of Holiness

We are called to live lives that honor God — in our worship, our relationships, and our conduct in the world.

Worship

Worship at Disciple Parish is heartfelt and reverent, combining prayer, hymns, and the Word of God as we gather together before the Lord.

What Makes Celestial Worship Distinctive?

Here are some of the Distinctive Worship Practices you may notice at Celestial Church of Christ.

Light & the Altar (Zechariah 4:1–2; Exodus 30:1–8)

Light at the altar is a longstanding part of Celestial worship, symbolizing the presence and glory of God among His people.

White Garments (Revelation 7:9)

Members and worshippers often wear white during services, a practice associated with purity and reverence before the Lord.

Prayer & Reverence (1 Timothy 2:8; John 4:24)

Prayer holds a central place in Celestial worship, offered with deep reverence for the presence of God in the sanctuary.

Barefoot Worship (Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15)

It is customary in Celestial Church of Christ to remove one's shoes within the sanctuary, a sign of reverence in God's presence.

Hymns & Worship (Ephesians 5:19; Psalm 150:3–5)

Hymns and worship songs are central to our services, uniting the congregation in praise.

This page is intentionally written at a general, welcoming level. More detail about our specific worship customs at Disciple Parish will be added here over time.

Our Christian Life

Faith at Disciple Parish is lived out daily, not only on Sunday.

Prayer

We are a people of prayer, seeking God together and individually in every season of life.

Fasting

Fasting, paired with prayer, is part of how our church seeks God's presence and direction.

Thanksgiving

We are called to give thanks to God in all things, recognizing His faithfulness in our lives.

Tithes & Offerings

Giving is an act of worship and trust in God's provision, and it sustains the ministry of the church.

Christian Conduct

We seek to live with integrity and love, reflecting Christ in how we treat one another and our neighbors.

Service to Others

We believe faith is lived out through serving one another and our community with the love of Christ.
