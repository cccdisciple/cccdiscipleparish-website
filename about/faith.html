About

Our Faith

Faith in Jesus Christ. Guided by the Holy Spirit. Rooted in Scripture.

At Disciple Parish, we are a Christian community centered on Jesus Christ and committed to worshipping God, growing in faith, and living lives that honor Him.

As Celestians, our faith is expressed through prayer, worship, Scripture, holiness, service, thanksgiving, and a continual desire to follow the leading of the Spirit of the Lord.

Faith in Jesus Christ

Jesus Christ is at the center of our faith. We seek to know Him, follow Him, and live according to His teachings.

Guided by the Holy Spirit

We seek the leading of the Spirit of the Lord in our worship, prayer, spiritual life, and daily walk with God.

Rooted in Scripture

Scripture provides an essential foundation for our faith and our understanding of Christian life and worship.

A Life of Holiness

We are called to live lives that honor God through holiness, prayer, discipline, love, service, and Christian conduct.

Worship

Worship is at the heart of our life together. Through prayer, Scripture, music, preaching, thanksgiving, giving, and other expressions of devotion, we gather to honor God and seek His presence.

Learn About Our Worship

What Makes Celestial Worship Distinctive?

Here are some of the Distinctive Worship Practices you may notice at Celestial Church of Christ.

Light & the Altar (Zechariah 4:1–2; Exodus 30:1–8)

Light at the altar is an established part of Celestial worship and a visible expression of reverence before God. During Sunday worship, the congregation remains standing while the seven candles on the altar are lit and the Inner Altar is sanctified. The Sanctuary also includes a burning lamp and other prescribed elements that help preserve the order and sacred atmosphere of worship.

For visitors, these practices may be unfamiliar at first, but they are part of the Church’s established worship order and its approach to God with reverence, prayer, and devotion.

The Sutana (SU-TA-NA) (Revelation 7:9)

The white prayer-and-worship garment worn by Celestians is called the Sutana (SU-TA-NA). It is worn together with barefoot worship and other related practices of reverence. Learn more about the Sutana.

Prayer & Reverence (1 Timothy 2:8; John 4:24)

Prayer holds a central place in Celestial worship, offered with deep reverence for the presence of God in the Sanctuary.

Barefoot Worship (Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:15)

In Celestial Church of Christ, worshippers remove their shoes within the Sanctuary as an expression of reverence before God.

Hymns & Worship (Ephesians 5:19; Psalm 150:3–5)

Hymns and worship songs are central to our services, uniting the congregation in praise.
