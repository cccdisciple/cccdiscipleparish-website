About

Celestial Church of Christ

Celestial Church of Christ is a Bible-believing Christian Church devoted to worship, prayer, holiness, and service to God.

Celestial Church of Christ is a Bible-believing Christian Church devoted to worship, prayer, holiness, and service to God. Our faith is centered on Jesus Christ, rooted in Scripture, and guided by the Holy Spirit.

Founded in 1947 in Porto-Novo, in present-day Benin in West Africa, through Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa, Celestial Church of Christ has grown into a worldwide Christian Church.

Visitors to a Celestial Church often notice its distinctive practices immediately: the white garment known as the Sutana (SU-TA-NA), barefoot worship, prayer and hymns, light at the altar, and an established order within the Sanctuary. Together, these form part of a larger Christian life centered on reverence before God, prayer, holiness, thanksgiving, service, and fellowship.

Our Beginning

Before the establishment of Celestial Church of Christ, Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa worked as a carpenter and timber trader, following the trade of his father. According to the Church's history, while in the forest of Dahomey—now the Republic of Benin—he experienced the events that would lead to the beginning of the Church.

Oshoffa spent forty days and forty nights in the wilderness, an experience the Church's account connects with the biblical account of Jesus Christ's forty days and forty nights in the wilderness.

During prayer on September 19, 1947, Oshoffa received a spiritual message concerning his mission: he was being sent to preach to people who, when faced with the difficulties of life, had turned away from Christ in search of other spiritual powers.

On September 29, 1947, the mission of Celestial Church of Christ began through him in Porto-Novo.

Our Founder — Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa

Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa was the founder and first Pastor of Celestial Church of Christ. His calling and ministry shaped the early spiritual life, worship, and development of the Church.

The Church's history remembers his ministry for prophecy and for works of healing and restoration. Oshoffa led Celestial Church of Christ for 38 years and died in 1985 at the age of 76.

The Name “Celestial Church of Christ”

The name Celestial Church of Christ was itself received as part of the Church's spiritual beginning. According to the Church's account, the name was revealed by the Holy Spirit through a Prophet who remained in a trance for seven days.

The Church likewise understands its Tenets and Mode of Worship as having been ordained by the Holy Spirit.

The Order of Celestial Worship

The distinctive order of Celestial worship also reaches back to the Church's earliest days. The Mode of Worship includes the setting of the Altar, seating within it, the number of candles used for different services, and the arrangement of worshippers.

One particularly significant account dates to Friday, October 5, 1947. According to the Church's history, a Prophetess under the influence of the Holy Spirit sketched the revealed seating arrangement in the wilderness, using oranges to represent the positions of worshippers.

Celestial Church of Christ also follows an established Order of Service for different occasions, including Marriage, Funeral, Naming Ceremony, and services in remembrance of those who have died.

A Worship Tradition That Continues Today

The spiritual order established in the Church's early history remains visible in Celestial worship today. Worship follows an Order of Service, the Sanctuary and Altar have an established order, and Celestians worship barefoot within the Sanctuary and while wearing the Sutana.

Prayer, Scripture, hymns, thanksgiving, holiness, and reverence before God remain central to the worship life of the Church.

A Worldwide Church

From its beginning in Porto-Novo, Celestial Church of Christ has grown to include Parishes and Dioceses throughout the world, with its International Headquarters in Nigeria.

The Pastor serves as Head of the Church. The Church's administrative structure includes Heads of Diocese, Regional Heads, District Heads or Supervisors, Zonal Heads, and Parish Shepherds. At the parish level, the Shepherd is responsible for the Parish and is assisted in its administration by the Parochial Committee.

The Mission Continues

Celestial Church of Christ continues its mission centered on Jesus Christ and the proclamation of the Gospel. The Church calls its members to worship God in holiness and reverence, share the Good News of Jesus Christ, grow in their knowledge of the faith, and prepare for the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ.

The Church's mission also includes working for Christian unity, strengthening homes and families through prayer, counseling, and spiritual ministry, supporting education, organizing charitable and relief work, and encouraging communities free from crime and drugs.
