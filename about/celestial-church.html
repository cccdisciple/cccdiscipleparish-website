About

Celestial Church of Christ

Disciple Parish is part of Celestial Church of Christ, a worldwide Christian denomination with roots in West Africa.

Our Beginning

Celestial Church of Christ was founded in 1947 in Porto-Novo, in what is now the Republic of Benin, West Africa.

Our Founder

The church was founded by Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa, who is recognized by Celestial Church of Christ as its founder and first pastor.

The Growth of the Church

From its beginning in West Africa, Celestial Church of Christ grew into a large denomination with congregations, or parishes, established across many countries.

A Worldwide Church

Today, Celestial Church of Christ has parishes around the world, including in the United States. Disciple Parish is one of those parishes, serving Hartford, Connecticut.

Our Faith

Celestial Church of Christ is a Christian church centered on faith in Jesus Christ, prayer, and worship guided by the Holy Spirit. Learn more on our Our Faith page.

Our Worship

Worship in Celestial Church of Christ carries distinctive traditions passed down since its founding, including the use of light at the altar, white garments, and reverent, barefoot worship. Learn more on our Our Faith page.
