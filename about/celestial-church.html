About

Celestial Church of Christ

Disciple Parish is a local parish of Celestial Church of Christ, a Christian church centered on faith in Jesus Christ.

Celestians seek to live their faith through worship, prayer, Scripture, holiness, service, thanksgiving, and spiritual life, guided by the Spirit of the Lord.

The Church also carries distinctive worship practices that visitors may encounter for the first time. We welcome you to learn more about these practices as you get to know our community.

Our Beginning

The Church was founded in 1947 in Porto-Novo, in what is now the Republic of Benin, West Africa.

Our Founder

The Church was founded by Samuel Bilehou Joseph Oshoffa, who is recognized as its founder and first pastor.

The Growth of the Church

From its beginning in West Africa, the Church grew into a large denomination with congregations, or parishes, established across many countries.

A Worldwide Church

Today, the Church has parishes around the world, including in the United States. Disciple Parish is one of those parishes, serving Hartford, Connecticut.

Our Faith

The Church is a Christian church centered on faith in Jesus Christ, prayer, and worship guided by the Holy Spirit. Learn more on our Our Faith page.

Our Worship

Worship carries distinctive practices passed down since the Church's founding, including the use of light at the altar, the Sutana (SU-TA-NA), and reverent, barefoot worship. Learn more on our Our Faith page.
