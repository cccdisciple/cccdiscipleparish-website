About

Living the Christian Life

Faith expressed through holiness, love, prayer, service, and daily devotion to God.

Christian life does not begin and end inside the Sanctuary. Faith is expressed in the way we live, the way we treat others, the choices we make, and the way we respond to God each day.

As Celestians, we seek to live our faith through prayer, holiness, spiritual discipline, thanksgiving, service, generosity, and Christian conduct.

A Life of Prayer

Prayer is part of the believer's ongoing relationship with God. Through prayer we give thanks, seek guidance, bring our concerns before God, and remain attentive to His presence.

Holiness

Holiness involves more than what happens during worship. It is reflected in the choices we make and the way we conduct ourselves. To pursue holiness is to continually seek to live in a manner that honors God.

The Condition of the Heart

Christian conduct begins within. Love, peace, humility, sincerity, self-control, forgiveness, and faithfulness shape the way we respond to God and to other people.

How We Treat One Another

A Christian life cannot be separated from the way we treat other people. Love, patience, forgiveness, compassion, honesty, and peace belong in our families, friendships, workplaces, and communities.

Attire: The Sutana

The white garment worn by Celestians is called the Sutana (SU-TA-NA). It is worn with reverence during worship and is part of the Church's distinctive attire.

The Sutana is worn together with a few related practices.

The Sutana (Revelation 3:4–5; Revelation 7:9–14; Revelation 19:8)

The Sutana is worn by Celestians during worship, set apart specifically for approaching God.

Barefoot Worship (Exodus 3:5; Joshua 5:13–15)

It is customary to remove one's shoes within the Sanctuary, and again once the Sutana is worn, as a sign of reverence in God's presence.

The Loin (Daniel 10:5; John 13:4–5; Revelation 1:13)

The loin is a related garment element worn alongside the Sutana as part of worship dress.

Head Covering (1 Corinthians 11:3–16)

Women cover their heads within the Sanctuary as part of this same practice of reverence.

Together, the Sutana, the loin, and worshipping barefoot are treated within the Church as established, longstanding elements of worship — not matters of personal preference or fashion, but part of how Celestians have historically approached God in the Sanctuary.

New to Celestial worship? You do not need to already own a Sutana to visit. Come as you are, and feel free to ask questions as you grow familiar with this practice.

Fasting & Spiritual Discipline

Spiritual discipline creates space for believers to seek God intentionally. Prayer and fasting are among the practices through which believers discipline themselves, seek God's direction, and deepen their dependence upon God.

Generosity & Stewardship

Christian stewardship includes how we use our resources, time, abilities, and opportunities. Giving is one expression of that stewardship, while generosity also appears through service, hospitality, compassion, and willingness to help others.

Understand Our Offerings

Faith That Serves

Christian life is not only about receiving from the Church. It is also about finding ways to serve God and serve others. Every person has something they can contribute through time, abilities, resources, encouragement, prayer, or practical service.

You do not need a title to serve.

Faith Beyond the Sanctuary

What happens in worship should shape what happens afterward.

The reverence we bring into the Sanctuary should influence how we speak.

The prayers we offer should influence how we respond to difficulty.

The gratitude we express should influence how we see what we have.

The love we receive from God should influence how we treat others.

Living as a Celestian

Being a Celestian is more than belonging to a congregation. It involves living a Christian life centered on faith in God, worship, prayer, holiness, service, and fellowship with other believers.
