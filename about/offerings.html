Understanding Our Offerings

Giving as an expression of worship, gratitude, faith, and stewardship.

Giving has an important place within the spiritual life of the Church. Offerings provide opportunities for Celestians to express gratitude, faith, generosity, and stewardship before God.

This page is educational. To give to Disciple Parish, visit our giving page.

Why We Give

Giving is more than a financial transaction. It can be an expression of worship, thanksgiving, obedience, generosity, and trust in God.

Celestial Church of Christ recognizes three distinct, established forms of offering: Seed Offering (Owo Igba), Tithe (Owo Idamewa), and Thanksgiving Offering (Ope). Each has its own name and its own place within worship — they are three separate expressions of giving, not three names for the same offering.

Seed Offering — Owo Igba

Seed Offering, known in the Church as Owo Igba, is an established form of giving made in faith. It is understood as an offering given with trust in God and with the biblical image of sowing and reaping in mind.

Tithe — Owo Idamewa

Tithe, known in the Church as Owo Idamewa, is the giving of a tenth. It has an established place within Sunday worship and is collected following the sermon as part of the order of the service.

Thanksgiving Offering — Ope

Thanksgiving Offering, known in the Church as Ope, is given in gratitude to God. It gives believers an opportunity to acknowledge His goodness, mercy, faithfulness, provision, protection, and blessings, and it has an established place in Sunday worship following collections and tithe.

Collections, Tithe & Thanksgiving in Sunday Worship

Giving has its own place within the order of Sunday worship. Following the sermon, the service proceeds to collections and tithe offering.

The tithe box itself is treated with particular reverence, described as the “Treasury of Heaven,” to be carried with the utmost respect. The Shepherd, elders, and wardens each have a role in this order: offerings are received under the Shepherd's instruction, prayer over the tithe is offered only by the Shepherd or someone delegated by him, and wardens assist the elders receiving offerings in collecting the trays.

Once collections and tithe are complete, those bringing a thanksgiving offering come forward with their offering trays, dancing and singing, from the entrance of the Sanctuary to the Church Shepherd.

Giving as an Expression of Worship

Giving is one expression of worship and stewardship. It may be approached with gratitude, sincerity, faith, and a willing heart. Christian generosity also extends beyond money through service, hospitality, encouragement, prayer, and practical care for others.
