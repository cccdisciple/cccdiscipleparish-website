Church Leadership & Administration

The Pastor serves as Head of the Church. The Church's administrative structure includes Heads of Diocese, Regional Heads, District Heads or Supervisors, Zonal Heads, and Parish Shepherds. At the parish level, the Shepherd is responsible for the Parish and is assisted in its administration by the Parochial Committee.

Pastor — Head of Church

The Pastor serves as Head of Celestial Church of Christ worldwide.

The Shepherd

Within a parish, the Shepherd represents the Pastor and serves as head of the parish, guiding the Parochial Committee and giving final approval before its decisions are carried out.

The Parochial Committee

The Parochial Committee is part of a parish's administration — a group of relatively senior, experienced members who assist and advise the Shepherd, without taking over the Shepherd's authority. No decision of the committee is implemented without the Shepherd's approval.

Ranks in Celestial Church of Christ

Celestial Church of Christ has an established system of ranks for its members. Male and female members follow separate rank progressions, with titles that distinguish the different ranks found within the Church.
