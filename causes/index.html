Serving Beyond Our Walls

Christian service does not end at the doors of the Sanctuary. As Celestians, we believe faith is lived out not only in worship, but in how we care for the people and communities around us.

Causes is where Disciple Parish documents that part of our life together — the practical ways we've served, and the ways we hope to continue serving, here in Hartford and, as God provides opportunity, abroad.

This area of our ministry is still developing. We're building this page honestly, one project at a time, rather than presenting a portfolio we don't yet have.

Our Community Work

Community work at Disciple Parish is still young, but it is real. Below are two initiatives we've undertaken recently. As we gather more documentation — photos, details, and outcomes — we'll expand these into fuller stories.

Community Health Drive

Disciple Parish recently held a community health drive as part of our ongoing effort to serve the practical needs of people in our community.

We're still gathering the full documentation for this initiative and will share a fuller account — including details of what took place — here as it becomes available.

Rummage / Garage Sale

Disciple Parish also recently held a rummage / garage sale as a community and fundraising initiative.

As with our health drive, a fuller account will be added here as that documentation becomes available.

Looking Ahead

As Disciple Parish continues to grow, we hope to take on additional community initiatives locally in Hartford, and, as God opens doors, projects abroad.

We don't yet have specific future causes to announce. We'd rather document this work honestly as it happens than describe a program that doesn't exist yet.

Get Involved

We're still developing how members and friends of Disciple Parish can formally take part in this area of ministry — through volunteering, giving, or other support. As those pathways take shape, we'll share them here.

In the meantime, if you'd like to learn more or offer support, we'd love to hear from you.
