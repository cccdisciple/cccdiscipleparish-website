Welcome to Disciple Parish

Whether you are visiting for the first time or have worshipped in Celestial Church of Christ for many years, we are honored to welcome you.

Disciple Parish is a Christ-centered church where people come together to worship God, grow in their faith, care for one another, and serve our community through the leading of the Holy Spirit.

Mission

Helping every person take their next step with God, experience His love, and leave forever changed by His presence.

Vision

To help people know Jesus Christ, grow in their faith, and share His love through worship, prayer, and service.

Foundation

Jesus Christ is at the center of everything we do. We worship, pray, study God's Word, and encourage one another to live lives that honor Him.
